Koleex AI interface cues — provenance

These MP3 files are the "glass" pack of UI SFX (https://github.com/romainsimon/uisfx),
whose audio is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Attribution is not
required; this notice is kept so the origin of what ships stays auditable.

Each file is named for the Koleex AI moment it plays on (see
src/lib/sounds/catalog.ts). Original cue names, for reference:
